Recorded scientific evidence
Results and replay values are selected from the audited frozen Step 09 simulation records. Traffic demand is synthetic and uncalibrated; the inferred crossings, junction arrangement and signal timings are experimental. The replay shows recorded aggregate queues and signal stages, not measured city traffic, individual trajectories, a live simulation or a geographic map.
