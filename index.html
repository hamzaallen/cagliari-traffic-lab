Model lifecycle

Track the experiment.
Keep the evidence.

Registration records a candidate. It does not make that candidate production-ready.

  1. Train

    360 episodes in the expanded study; three learner seeds.

  2. Validate & register

    90 validation runs; 454 audited tracking records; no promoted aliases.

  3. Freeze & evaluate

    210 held-out runs; no exploration, updates or selection on test outcomes.

  4. Audit & decide

    126,000 trace frames reconciled; 8,400 policy choices replayed; pressure retained.

Coverage is not performance.

Q7 used pressure fallback for 11.78% of regular decisions and 13.33% of stress decisions. All three learners remain experimental. Improving table coverage alone does not establish better traffic control.

What this study does—and does not—establish

One historical experimental junction. Piazza d'Armi changed to an experimental roundabout in September 2026; this signalized model does not represent its current operation. Demand is synthetic. This is not calibrated citywide traffic, a field trial or a road-safety certification.

A finite horizon. Waiting is accrued through 600 seconds. Active and pending traffic remain in the accounting; future waiting is unmeasured.

Conditional evidence. Intervals describe demand variation for these frozen policies. They do not estimate general Q-learning performance or uncertainty over arbitrary training runs.

Consumed test seeds. Training, validation, test and stress seeds have now been used. New tuning requires a new evaluation design.

These downloads are a sanitized public derivative, not the full simulation archive. Full reproduction also needs the retained code, checkpoints, network inputs and environment. Provenance and export scope · All 24 recorded replays · Rights and attribution
